Never put four stats in a row (big number + caption, repeated four times across). Sydney called it "a tell of a claude created design and massively overused."

**Why:** It reads as generic, templated AI design, and her project is a design-school data viz final.

**How to apply:** When several numbers matter, show one at a time with a picker, fold them into a chart or track, or write them as one sentence. This applies to any count of identical stat tiles, not only four. Related: [[less-text-more-widgets]].
