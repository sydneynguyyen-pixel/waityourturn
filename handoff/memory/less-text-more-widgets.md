Sydney's pages should feel like a student-friendly educational site: each section is its own box or widget, with little text, few levels of hierarchy and lots of white space. Long notes go behind a toggle.

**Why:** Class feedback and her own review said the pages were "a lot of overwhelming text" with "not enough white space."

**How to apply:** Default to an interactive widget (picker, stepper, flip card, slider) over a paragraph. Give each box one title and one short line. Related: [[no-four-stat-rows]].
